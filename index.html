Understanding postprandial metabolic trajectories

Multivariate analysis of longitudinal metabolomics data to characterise temporal profiles and explore differences between individuals.

  • Mixed models / LMMS
  • TimeOmics
  • Clustering
  • Python · R
Read the research overview

Scientific question

How can we summarise the dynamics of many metabolic signals measured repeatedly after a meal, and identify groups of similar temporal profiles?

Approach

Longitudinal modelling with LMMS, multivariate integration with TimeOmics, and clustering with PAM using Wasserstein and Jensen–Shannon distances. Bootstrap resampling was used to assess cluster stability, including ARI and Jaccard comparisons.

My contribution

I worked on the analysis workflow, the representation and clustering of temporal profiles and individuals, and interactive visualisations using Plotly. Python and R were connected through rpy2. Scientific supervision: Etienne Jules, at the Human Nutrition Unit (UNH).

Public materials. This is a methodological summary of my internship. The original analysis notebooks and biological data are not included in this public repository.

Scientific question

How can statistical models account for temporal variation and uncertainty when analysing building energy performance?

Approach

Exploration of generalised linear and hierarchical models, ARIMA/ARMAX models, Gaussian processes and hidden Markov models, with probabilistic implementation in PyMC.

My contribution

Research internship at LOCIE, supervised by Simon Rouchier and Olivier Bodart, as part of my Master’s in Applied Mathematics and Machine Learning for Engineering.

Public materials. My research notebooks for this internship are available in the branch poem · my_code. It is a fork of Simon Rouchier’s repository; authorship and history remain in the repository.
